On-body photo setup

Take every on-body shot the same way so pieces line up side by side on your outfit boards.

  1. Pick one spot with a plain wall and mark where you stand with tape.
  2. Put your phone at waist height, about 7 ft away, in the same place every time. Use the 10-second timer.
  3. Fit your whole body in frame, head to shoes, with a little space above and below.
  4. Face the camera, arms slightly away from your sides, same daylight each time.
  5. Wear plain black or neutral pieces for everything you're not photographing.

Measurements & sizes

Add a few measurements and Rotation works out your size in US, UK, EU, Japanese and Korean sizing, then shows it on your next buys. Everything is optional.

How to measure
  • Chest / bust: around the fullest part, tape level under your arms.
  • Waist: where you wear your pants, not your natural waist, for jeans sizing.
  • Hips: around the widest part of your seat, feet together.
  • Inseam: crotch to the floor, barefoot, or measure a pair of pants that fits.
  • Foot: stand on paper, mark heel and longest toe, measure in cm.
