Starter kits & fit

Buy these first. Five pieces per style covers most outfits.

Grisch starter kit

  1. Navy down vest (Uniqlo or Patagonia)
  2. Grey or navy crewneck or quarter-zip (Ralph Lauren or Massimo Dutti)
  3. Light-wash straight jeans (Levi's 501 or Nudie)
  4. White leather sneakers (Koio or Axel Arigato)
  5. Tissot PRX or Seiko Presage

Fit: knits fitted at the shoulder, jeans straight with a small stack at the shoe.

Streetwear starter kit

  1. Two heavyweight tees (Camber or LA Apparel)
  2. Reverse Weave hoodie (Champion)
  3. Double-knee or wide work pants (Carhartt or Dickies)
  4. One statement sneaker (Samba, 550 or Gel-1130)
  5. Fitted cap or G-Shock

Fit: size up one in tees for a boxy look; wide pants balance chunkier shoes.

Cozy starter kit

  1. Fleece (Patagonia Synchilla or Better Sweater)
  2. Full-zip hoodie (American Giant)
  3. Tapered joggers (Vuori or Uniqlo)
  4. Birkenstock Boston or Wicked Good slippers
  5. Darn Tough wool socks

Fit: tapered bottoms and a slightly relaxed top look put-together, not sloppy.

How to tell quality

  • Tees: 6 oz or heavier cotton, tight collar ribbing
  • Knits: 100% wool, merino or cashmere, not acrylic blends
  • Down: 600+ fill power
  • Shoes: full-grain leather; boots with a Goodyear welt can be resoled
  • Check for repair programs and lifetime warranties

Price tiers are approximate and shift with sales and seasons. For luxury pieces, buy only through authenticated resale.

On-body photo setup

Take every on-body shot the same way so pieces line up side by side on your outfit boards.

  1. Pick one spot with a plain wall and mark where you stand with tape.
  2. Put your phone at waist height, about 7 ft away, in the same place every time. Use the 10-second timer.
  3. Fit your whole body in frame, head to shoes, with a little space above and below.
  4. Face the camera, arms slightly away from your sides, same daylight each time.
  5. Wear plain black or neutral pieces for everything you're not photographing.
